QUESTIONS

Good to know.

What does ByNeo Studio do?

We design and build websites for South African businesses. We also do brand identity (logos, colours and type) and digital creative such as posters, social media graphics and banners.

Where are you based?

Boksburg, Gauteng. We work with businesses across South Africa, so you don't need to be nearby.

Who do you work with?

Businesses with real customers whose online presence is missing, outdated or doesn't match their reputation: clinics, consultants, trades and local services. New businesses setting up properly are welcome too.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your business needs. Before any work starts you get a short quote with two options and the price up front.

Can you redesign the website I already have?

Yes. Send us the link and we'll tell you what we'd change first.
