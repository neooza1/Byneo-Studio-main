01 / FEATURED PROJECT

Mokwatedi
Dental Clinic.

A website for an established dental practice in Cosmo City, built to make a stronger first impression online and make booking easy.

mokwatedidental.co.za
01
01

The Challenge

The goal was to create a stronger digital first impression for a dental clinic whose online presence needed to better communicate professionalism, trust and quality of care.

02

The Approach

We focused on clear information architecture, stronger visual hierarchy, intentional typography and a patient journey that makes it easier to understand the practice and take the next step.

03

The Outcome

A modern, responsive experience that gives the practice a more credible digital presence while making important information easier for prospective patients to find.

Real patient Google reviews, built into the site so new patients see them before they book.
