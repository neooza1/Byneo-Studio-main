01 / FEATURED PROJECT

R Masilo
Attorneys.

A new identity for a law firm in Germiston: attorneys, notaries and conveyancers. One idea, carried from the logo onto paper and onto the walls.

01

The Challenge

The firm needed a look that matches how seriously it takes its work, and that stays consistent on a business card, a letter and the sign outside the office.

02

The Idea

Notaries seal what they sign, so the seal became the brand. A raised rose-gold seal around an RM monogram, in navy and rose gold: serious, trusted and clearly theirs.

03

The Delivery

Logo in 3D and flat versions, business cards with a scan-to-save QR code, a Word letterhead the team can type straight into, and office signage designed for production with KMJ Signage.

02 / WEBSITE · LIVE

Mokwatedi
Dental Clinic.

A website for an established dental practice in Cosmo City, built to make a stronger first impression online and make booking easy.

mokwatedidental.co.za
02
01

The Challenge

The clinic had loyal patients, but someone finding it online for the first time couldn't see how good the practice is or how to book.

02

The Approach

Clear services, real Google reviews from patients, before-and-after results, and a booking button that is always one tap away on a phone.

03

The Outcome

A live website at mokwatedidental.co.za that looks as professional as the clinic, works well on mobile, and makes booking easy for new patients.
